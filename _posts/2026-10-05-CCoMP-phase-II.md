title: C-CoMP Phase II kickoff
---

The [Center for Chemical Currencies of a Microbial Planet (C-CoMP)](https://ccomp-stc.org) held its annual meeting October 5-7, 2026 to kick off Phase II of the center.

As part of this next phase, our lab is building a multiscale simulation framework that connects biogeophysical models with microbial models across an ocean water column, integrating physical and chemical dynamics with microbial physiology and community interactions.

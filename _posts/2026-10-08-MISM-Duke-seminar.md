title: Eran visits Duke as a MISM seminar speaker
---

Eran visited Duke University on October 8-9, 2026 as a [Multiscale Immune Systems Modeling (MISM)](https://immunescale.org) seminar speaker, and taught a master class on Vivarium.

MISM is setting up an open simulation ecosystem for immunology, and we look forward to exploring how the Vivarium and compositional systems biology approach can support multiscale immune modeling.
